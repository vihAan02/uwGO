## Cross-check against UW's Campus Accessibility pages

`connections.ts` lists 38 building-to-building links stated on the university's accessibility
pages. 29 of them are direct links in the network. 6 more are not drawn as a direct
link but the two buildings are joined under cover through others (the pages name a tunnel by the buildings
it serves, the survey draws it segment by segment):

- MC–DC (bridge): "Overpass on DC second floor to MC"
- QNC–SLC (bridge): "Overpass to the SLC on the QNC first floor, North end"
- EV1–TC (tunnel): "Lower-level tunnel near EV1 room 109"
- ML–TC (tunnel): "Tunnel between ML, AL and TC"
- ML–HH (tunnel): "Tunnel, stairs involved (not step-free)"
- ML–SCH (tunnel): "Tunnel, stairs involved (not step-free)"

The remaining 3 are **not in the network at all**, and the network follows its source rather
than adding them, because a bridge drawn where none stands is worse than none. Someone should check these
on the ground and, if they exist, they belong in the survey upstream:

- DC–M3 (bridge): "Automated overpass on DC third floor to M3"
- NH–LIB (tunnel): "Tunnel from Needles Hall Southeast end to the Dana Porter (Arts) library"
- PAS–EV2 (link): "Link at the North end of PAS to EV2"

Links the network has that the accessibility pages do not state (6):
