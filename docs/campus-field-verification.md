## How the ranking works

**What is routed.** Three kinds of routing, with every trip weighted by its two places:

- Campus decisions: the campus-aware walk decision (`src/engine/campusRoute.ts`) for every ordered pair of the 48 places (the network's buildings, and residences off it), made against a stand-in for Google's walk: the straight line, 30% longer, at 1.33 m/s. No routing API is asked. 2256 trips get a decision; what counts is where UW Go sends a student somewhere other than that walk.
- Winter routes, and step-free winter routes, between every ordered pair of network buildings (1560 trips).

All of it on a Tuesday at noon in term, inside every opening window, so the ranking is about the network rather than the clock. The stand-in cannot know Google's real walking times, so campus decisions here show corrections (every walk into PAC) rather than shortcuts, which depend on those times.

**Demand.** A trip weighs the product of its two places' weights. A network building weighs its size, taken from its surveyed corridor length relative to the median (×0.5 to ×2); a residence off the network weighs ×1. Each then takes every role UW Go's own data gives it. UW Go has no campus-wide timetable of rooms, so these roles are the whole of it:

- ×2: the gym UW Go plans workouts around
- ×1.5: the Student Life Centre: open all hours, and the way into PAC
- ×1.5: a library UW Go offers for gaps between classes
- ×1.5: a residence or college residence, where students' days start and end
- ×1.25: classrooms UW Go has floor-plan room positions for

**Figures for each target.**

- Reliance: the trips whose route uses it (for campus decisions, the trips UW Go sends through it instead of Google's walk), and their share of weighted demand.
- Losing it: each such trip decided or routed again with it closed, and how much worse it becomes, averaged over all weighted demand. Campus decisions are measured in seconds. Winter and step-free routes are measured as those routes are chosen: seconds, with each second outside counted 4 times. A trip left with no allowed way in (campus) or no route (winter, step-free) counts 300.
- Confirming it: for a quarantined or restricted link, winter and step-free routes with it usable both ways; for a change of floor, step-free routes with a step-free elevator there. The improvement in the same measure, averaged over all weighted demand; a trip that gains a route counts 300.
- Traffic: the share of weighted demand starting or ending at any building it touches (both of a link's buildings). For a rule about a building's way in or out, the share of weighted demand whose walk UW Go corrects on that rule's word; for a building's hours, the share whose campus decision or winter route passes through the building, each trip counted once.

**Factors**, each 0 to 1 relative to the target that scores highest on it:

- routing: the largest of, for each kind of routing (campus ×1, winter ×0.5, step-free ×0.3), half its reliance and half the cost of losing it; for a rule, its corrected share; for hours, the routing that passes through the building, each trip weighted as the heaviest kind of routing that does, ×0.25, since unknown hours only change routes before 07:00 and after 22:00;
- traffic; shortcut: the winter improvement if confirmed; accessibility: the larger of the step-free improvement if confirmed and half the step-free reliance on something whose access nothing routing relies on records.

**Uncertainty**, 0 to 1, in two parts. For using it: by evidence (official 0.3, field verified 0.05, corroborated 0.45, surveyed 0.6, anecdotal 0.75, inferred 0.8, unresolved 1), except 0.3 for a surveyed stairwell, which rarely turns out not to be there; plus 0.15 for each disagreement between sources and 0.1 for a check a reviewer marked urgent. For its access: 1 for a change of floor nobody has confirmed step-free, 0.8 for a door or link whose access nothing routing relies on records, otherwise by evidence.

**Score** = 100 × (0.5 × routing × uncertainty for using it + 0.1 × traffic × uncertainty for using it + 0.2 × shortcut + 0.2 × accessibility × uncertainty about access).

**Tiers.**

- P0: not field-verified, and either critical (a rule UW Go corrects walks with, or without it a corrected walk has no allowed way in), or UW Go sends at least 0.30% of weighted demand through it instead of Google's walk while a reviewer has flagged it, sources disagree about it or its evidence is no better than surveyed, or a score of 30 or more.
- P1: a score of 12 or more; or not field-verified, and UW Go sends any trip through it instead of Google's walk, or routing, accessibility or shortcut, each with its uncertainty, reaches 0.3.
- P2: a score of 4 or more, campus and winter reliance on at least 0.20% of weighted demand, a disagreement between sources, or a reviewer's check of priority 1 or 2.
- P3: everything else.

**What it cannot see.** Google's real walking times, and so which shortcuts UW Go would take against them; trips from anywhere other than the network's buildings and residences; when in the day trips are made; and any door or link the survey never drew.
